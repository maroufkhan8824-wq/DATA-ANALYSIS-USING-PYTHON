NETFLIX BUSINESS INSIGHTS REPORT
=============================================

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
The dataset contains 8,790 unique titles.
Movies: 6,126 (69.69%).
TV Shows: 2,664 (30.31%).

2. CONTENT TYPE INSIGHTS
Movies represent the larger share of the catalog.

3. GEOGRAPHIC INSIGHTS
Leading country by title count: United States (3,240 titles).
Titles associated with multiple countries are counted once for each associated country.

4. GENRE INSIGHTS
Most common listed genre: International Movies (2,752 titles).

5. CONTENT RATING INSIGHTS
Most common rating category: TV-MA (3,205 titles).

6. RELEASE YEAR INSIGHTS
Year with the highest title count: 2018 (1,146 titles).
Recent release years may be incomplete in the dataset.

7. RECOMMENDATIONS
- Monitor the Movies and TV Shows content mix.
- Explore genre gaps in important markets.
- Compare rating categories when planning audience segments.
- Track release-year trends while accounting for incomplete years.
- Use viewer engagement and subscription data before making investment decisions.

8. DATA LIMITATIONS
- This dataset describes catalog titles, not actual viewership.
- Country and genre counts may overlap.
- Ratings do not directly measure audience demand.
- Missing values may affect individual analyses.
